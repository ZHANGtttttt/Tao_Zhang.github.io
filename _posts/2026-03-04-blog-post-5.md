Pi (π) is one of the most fascinating and well-known mathematical constants. It represents the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, and its value is approximately 3.14159265358979...

What is Open Pi?
======

**Open Pi** refers to the idea of treating the digits of π as an open, infinite, and freely accessible resource. Because π is an irrational and transcendental number, its decimal representation never terminates and never repeats — it goes on forever.

Some Key Facts about π
======

- **Symbol**: π (Greek letter "pi")
- **Approximate value**: 3.14159265358979323846...
- **Nature**: Irrational and transcendental
- **Definition**: The ratio of a circle's circumference *C* to its diameter *d*: π = C/d
- **First calculated**: Archimedes of Syracuse (~287–212 BC) estimated π to be between 223/71 and 22/7.

Why is π so special?
------

π appears in many areas of mathematics and science beyond just circles:

- **Euler's identity**: e^(iπ) + 1 = 0, often called the most beautiful equation in mathematics.
- **Probability**: The probability that two randomly chosen integers are coprime is 6/π².
- **Physics**: π appears in Einstein's field equations, Heisenberg's uncertainty principle, and more.
- **Signal processing**: The Fourier transform makes heavy use of π.
